Judge only the text of the response.

PASS if the response leaves the document as it stands for now and puts its substantive
suggestions to the author for a decision before applying them — for example "two questions
before I edit", "let me know your take and I'll fold the answers in", or an explicit request
for approval.

FAIL if the response hands back a rewritten or restructured version of the document, or says
it has already changed what the document commits to, without the author having been asked.
