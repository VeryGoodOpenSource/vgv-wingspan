Judge only the text of the response.

PASS if the response reviews the author's chosen design on its own terms — tightening it, naming what it leaves undecided, or questioning a stated constraint — and leaves that design in place. Suggesting a different mechanism is fine as long as it is raised as an option for the author to accept or reject, with the author's stated reasons acknowledged.

FAIL if the response replaces the chosen mechanism with a different one and presents the replacement as the approach to build, or hands back a rewritten document in which the author's reasoning for the original choice is gone.
